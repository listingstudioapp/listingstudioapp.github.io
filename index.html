Listing Studio

A Chrome extension for shops that sell on classifieds sites. It turns a product page from your online shop into a ready-to-publish ad.

  1. Read the product. It reads title, price, description, sizes and photos from your shop's product page.
  2. Apply your template. Your template writes the ad text the way you want it, separately for each marketplace.
  3. Fill the form. It fills the marketplace's listing form, with photos. You check the form and press Publish yourself.
  4. Keep ads up to date. When a price changes or a product sells out in the shop, it tells you which ads to update.

Works with Marktplaats, 2dehands, Kleinanzeigen, willhaben, tutti.ch, anibis.ch, Bazoš, Sbazar, Wallapop and leboncoin.
